Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hosam AI Lab Publisher (the "App") handles information.

1. Summary

The App is a private tool used only by its owner to publish his own original AI-generated videos to his own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share personal data about anyone.

2. Information the App uses

  • The owner's account access tokens, granted through the platform's official authorization flow, used only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to check their publishing status.
  • The owner's basic account information (such as username and account ID) returned by the platform, used only to confirm which account a video is posted to.

The App does not access, collect or store data about other users, viewers, followers or commenters.

3. Storage

Tokens and settings are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server, database or third party operated by the App.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared. Videos and captions are sent only to the platform the owner chooses to publish to, through that platform's official API.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as the owner uses the App and can be deleted at any time by removing them from the owner's computer or revoking the App's access in the platform's settings.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its owner.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
